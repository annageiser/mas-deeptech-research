HOW TO FILL THIS SHEET
======================

Open gold-sheet.csv in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.
Fill the five right-hand columns for every row, then save as CSV
(keep the same column headers) and run:

    python -m eval_app.qda sheet-import gold-sheet.csv \
        --out data/gold/labels.yaml

You are the reference standard here. Judge each signal only from the
title, summary, evidence quote and source URL in front of you. The
sheet deliberately does not tell you which system found the signal or
how it labelled it, so that your labels measure the systems rather
than your agreement with them. Open the URL if the quote is unclear.

COLUMNS
-------
gold_keep           true / false
                    Is this a real, dated, actor-relevant quantum
                    signal that belongs in the corpus at all?
                    false = noise, boilerplate, wrong topic, undated.
                    If false, leave the two label columns blank.

gold_signal_type    one of:
                      legitimacy
                      customer_cocreation
                      community_ecosystem
                      future_trajectory

gold_dimension      one of the nineteen:
                      leadership_expertise
                      patents
                      publications
                      awards
                      testimonials
                      educational_outreach
                      funding_event
                      regulatory_recognition
                      collaborations_applications
                      pilots_pocs
                      customer_training
                      cloud_platform_listings
                      hpc_collaborations
                      industry_partnerships
                      academic_partnerships
                      roadmaps
                      milestones
                      technological_advances
                      long_horizon_claims

gold_actor_correct  true / false
                    Is the signal really about the named actor?
                    false = it is about someone else.

notes               free text, optional. Worth a line whenever the
                    call was close — that is the material for the
                    limitations section.

PACING
------
Budget about two minutes per row. Do not agonise: your first
defensible reading is the one to record. If a row is genuinely
ambiguous, pick the closest label and say why in notes.

SECOND CODER
------------
For an inter-rater reliability figure, have a second person label
10-15 of the same rows in a fresh copy, import it to a different
path, then:

    python -m eval_app.qda compare data/gold/labels.yaml other.yaml

That reports Cohen's kappa, which is the statistic to quote.
